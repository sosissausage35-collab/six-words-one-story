# Six Words, One Story

Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Six Words, One Story"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deranalabs-six-words-one-story-f982e410) ($5, top 3 split $2 / $2 / $1).

`main.ts` (Node.js) writes one complete story in exactly six words to `/output/entry.txt`:

> He buried the violin with her.

A widower lays his late wife's violin to rest with her — one life implied in six words: the music, the marriage, the grief, and the choice to bury the instrument instead of ever playing it again. What is left unsaid — who she was, what the violin meant, why it must be buried — is for the reader to fill in.

The program validates the story before writing: exactly 6 words, 1–60 characters, not only whitespace.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
